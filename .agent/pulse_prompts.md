1. **BODMAS & Arithmetic Traps**: Order of operations precedence traps (e.g. division before multiplication, implicit brackets like 6 ÷ 2(1+2), zero multiplication rules, or missing operators).

6. **Odd One Out & Pattern Breakers**: 4 or 5 numbers/words where exactly one violates a hidden rule (e.g., prime squares, palindromes, divisibility).
