Q. You have two opaque bags. Bag A contains 2 red marbles and 1 blue marble. Bag B contains 1 red marble and 2 blue marbles. You randomly choose a bag and then randomly draw one marble. If the marble drawn is red, what is the probability you chose Bag A?
Series:
Bag A: 2 Red, 1 Blue
Bag B: 1 Red, 2 Blue
Randomly choose bag, draw 1 marble.
Marble is RED.
P(Chose Bag A | Marble is Red) = ?

a. 1/2
b. 1/3
c. 2/3
d. 3/4

Correct Answer: c
