# Pulse Vector — YouTube Upload Details & Channel Strategy

This document serves as the official operational guide and upload tracking log for the **Pulse Vector** YouTube Channel.

---

## 🎯 1. Channel Positioning & Brand Identity

* **Channel Name**: Pulse Vector
* **Channel Tagline**: "Where Ancient History meets Modern Knowledge"
* **Channel Bio / About**:
  > "Pulse Vector — Where Ancient History meets Modern Knowledge. Master the epic history of the Mahabharat through high-quality 9:16 animated shorts, and stay ahead with concise Daily, Weekly & Monthly Current Affairs for exams and general awareness."

---

## 🔗 2. Related Video Setting (Shorts to Long-Form Binging)

To maximize viewer retention and cross-content binging between Shorts and long-form videos:

* **For Daily Current Affairs Shorts (9:16)**:
  * In YouTube Studio, set the **"Related video"** field to the **latest "Weekly Current Affairs Masterclass"** (or the previous Daily Current Affairs episode if no new Weekly episode exists).
* **For Weekly / Monthly Current Affairs Videos**:
  * Include an end-screen link and pinned description link to the **Pulse Vector Channel Homepage** & the official **Current Affairs Playlist**.

---

## 📌 3. Standard Pinned Comment Template

Immediately after uploading any Current Affairs Short/Video, post and pin the following comment:

```text
🧠 Master your exam prep with daily 60-second Current Affairs updates! 
📚 Watch our full Weekly Masterclass: [Link]
🔔 Subscribe to Pulse Vector & turn on notifications so you never miss a day!
Which question should we feature in tomorrow's quiz? Drop it below! 👇
```

*(Note: `[Link]` is dynamically substituted at upload time with the actual YouTube link of the latest Weekly Masterclass, e.g. `https://youtu.be/<video_id>`, or the official channel playlist URL `https://www.youtube.com/@PulseVector`.)*

---

## 🗓️ 4. Community Tab Engagement Calendar

Daily automated or manual posts scheduled to boost subscriber engagement:

* **8:00 AM IST**: Daily Current Affairs Quiz Poll (4 options tailored for UPSC / SSC / Govt Exam preparation).
* **1:00 PM IST**: Daily Top 5 Headlines Summary Infographic card.
